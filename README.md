## About

Biology textbooks show organs as flat drawings. BioReality lets you rotate, zoom and walk around a 3D **brain**, **heart** and **lungs**, then place them in your room with augmented reality. Each organ comes with an explanation of how it works and of the conditions that affect it, including their risks and complications.

I built it in Swift Playgrounds as my submission to the Swift Student Challenge 2023, and it was selected as a winner.

## Built with

- **SwiftUI** for the interface, onboarding and carousel
- **SceneKit** for the interactive 3D models
- **ARKit** for placing the models in the real world

## Run it

Open `Tequila.swiftpm` in **Swift Playgrounds** on iPad or Mac, or in **Xcode**, and run it on an iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later. Augmented reality needs a real device with a camera.
